About this project

Khandwa Recycles is an independent, citizen-led awareness project. It is not an official website of the Khandwa Municipal Corporation, the Government of Madhya Pradesh, or any government body, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

City-level figures are drawn from publicly available official sources, including the Swachh Survekshan 2024–25 results, municipal solid waste records, and CPCB publications.

Neighbourhood-level figures for the eight super-zones are illustrative estimates based on observed service patterns and local field notes. They are not official ward-level statistics and should not be treated as such.

This site is provided for information and awareness only. If you spot an error or have better local data, please reach out — the map improves when residents correct it.
